🏦 RBI Monetary Policy Instruments · Effect · Tricks

⬇️ महंगाई कम करनी है Money Supply ↓
अगर RBI महंगाई कम करना चाहती है (Money Supply ↓)
तो RBI ये करेगी:
Instrument ↑Effect
CRR ↑ Loan ↓ → Money Supply ↓
SLR ↑ Loan ↓ → Money Supply ↓
Bank Rate ↑ Loan महंगा → Money Supply ↓
Repo Rate ↑ Loan महंगा → Money Supply ↓
Reverse Repo Rate ↑ बैंक RBI के पास ज्यादा पैसा रखेंगे → Money Supply ↓
OMO (Government Securities बेचता है) Money Supply ↓
⬆️ अर्थव्यवस्था में पैसा बढ़ाना है Money Supply ↑
अगर RBI अर्थव्यवस्था में पैसा बढ़ाना चाहती है (Money Supply ↑)
तो RBI ये करेगी:
Instrument ↓Effect
CRR ↓ Loan ↑ → Money Supply ↑
SLR ↓ Loan ↑ → Money Supply ↑
Bank Rate ↓ Loan सस्ता → Money Supply ↑
Repo Rate ↓ Loan सस्ता → Money Supply ↑
Reverse Repo Rate ↓ बैंक RBI में कम पैसा रखेंगे → Money Supply ↑
OMO (Government Securities खरीदता है) Money Supply ↑

🚀 एक लाइन की ट्रिक

⬆️ जो भी Rate (CRR, SLR, Repo, Bank Rate, Reverse Repo) ↑ होगा
→ Money Supply ↓
⬇️ जो भी Rate ↓ होगा
→ Money Supply ↑
OMO उल्टा याद रखना:
RBI Bond खरीदे (Buy) → Money ↑
RBI Bond बेचे (Sell) → Money ↓

📣 5 सेकंड का याद रखने वाला मंत्र

"Rate बढ़े = Loan घटे = Money घटे"
"Rate घटे = Loan बढ़े = Money बढ़े"
"OMO Buy = Money Inject, OMO Sell = Money Withdraw."
